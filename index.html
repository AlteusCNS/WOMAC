Pain Relief Within Reach

Patient questionnaire for assessing pain, stiffness and physical function.

Instructions for patients

  1. Enter your name, gender and age in the Patient Information section.
  2. Answer all 24 questions about the affected joint, based on how you are feeling today.
  3. For each question, select only one number (0, 1, 2, 3 or 4) that best describes your pain, stiffness or difficulty.
  4. Use the same number scale for every question: 0, 1, 2, 3 or 4.
  5. After answering every question, select Calculate Score & View Report.
  6. Select Download PDF to download your report.

Response scale: 0 = None, 1 = Mild, 2 = Moderate, 3 = Severe, 4 = Extreme. Higher scores indicate more symptoms or difficulty. There are no right or wrong answers.
